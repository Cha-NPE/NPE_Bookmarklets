DG Label Jobs – bookmarklets

Install: drag the buttons you need onto your bookmarks bar:

DG Copy (Outlook)Manawatu DGsTaranaki DGs

Use

  1. In Outlook on the web, open the DG Jobs mailbox → your region's folder and click DG Copy (Outlook). It collects every DG Labelling request subject in the folder and copies them. To copy only some, select 2 or more emails first (Ctrl+click).
  2. In simPRO click DG Label Jobs, then Paste & check (allow clipboard access the first time). Anything already in simPRO is skipped.
  3. Click Create jobs, then use Copy DGS LIST rows and Copy DGS.docx pages as before.
