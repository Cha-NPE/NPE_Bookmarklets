DG Label Jobs – bookmarklets

Install: drag the buttons you need onto your bookmarks bar:

DG Copy (Outlook)Manawatu DGsTaranaki DGs

Use

  1. In Outlook on the web, open the DG Jobs mailbox → your region's folder and click DG Copy (Outlook). It collects every DG Labelling request subject in the folder and copies them. To copy only some, select 2 or more emails first (Ctrl+click).
  2. In simPRO click DG Label Jobs, then Paste & check (allow clipboard access the first time). Anything already in simPRO is skipped.
  3. Click Create jobs, then use Copy DGS LIST rows and Copy DGS.docx pages as before.

Network Ready (NR) Jobs – bookmarklets

Install: drag the buttons you need onto your bookmarks bar:

Manawatu & Wairarapa
PowerCo JobNR Step 2

Taranaki & Whanganui
Taranaki JobTaranaki NR Setup

Tauranga & Mt Maunganui / Coromandel & South Waikato
Tauranga JobTauranga NR Setup

Use

  1. In simPRO, open Jobs → Create New → Service Job and paste the NR Formatter output into Description.
  2. Click PowerCo Job (or Taranaki Job). It fills in the customer, cost centre, contact, salesperson/project manager, due date, job name, order number, tag and custom fields (WO number, CIWR, ICP, PowerCo Project Manager, Region), then removes the job name and order number lines from the Description. Taranaki Job always uses Taranaki & Whanganui, order 4100000590, Catherine Mallett and Tremaine Jones. Tauranga Job (use the Tauranga Tools NR Formatter output) sets Project Manager Paul Cooper, and reads EBP or EVL straight after the CIWR in the job name to set Cost Centre, Region and Order Number: EBP = Tauranga & Mt Maunganui, 4100000591; EVL = Coromandel & South Waikato, 4100000588.
  3. Set the Site yourself, then click Next.
  4. On the job, click NR Step 2 (or Taranaki NR Setup). It moves the original SAP text to Notes, unticks Auto Adjust, adds the take-off matching the tag, the call out fees and travel labour, locks items & price and applies the Network Ready folders, then saves. NR Step 2 also attaches the As-Built and Test Cert forms, creates the J A Russell PO ($100 Estimate) and puts its number into the Description. Tauranga NR Setup copies the Description from APPLICANT DETAILS down into Notes (the Description keeps it), uses the EBP or EVL travel labour and Network Ready take-off for the job's Region, attaches only the 360S014EA As-Built Low Voltage Connection Record (v14), and does not create a PO.
  5. Watch the blue bar at the top; if anything goes wrong it stops, doesn't save, and lists what to check.
